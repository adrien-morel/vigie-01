Product briefing

VIGIE-01

A defence & export-control watch system, scoped and built as a real deliverable — a pipeline working end to end, not a mock-up.

LangGraph · StateGraph Claude Haiku FastAPI · React V1 — tested end to end

Summary

Cut the daily synthesis time without giving up traceability

  • The observation. The hard part is not collecting — the tools exist — but classifying a heterogeneous stream consistently, cross-checking it, and being able to justify after the fact why a piece of information was kept.
  • The answer. A five-node pipeline where every statement is backed by a quote verified verbatim, and where bounded agentic escalation is triggered by a measured condition — the existence of an antecedent to cross-check against — and not by the article's category.
  • The state. V1 working end to end, the first slice of the V2 verifier and the first stage of V3 longitudinal reasoning (event threads) shipped, guardrails coded and tested both ways — not merely declared. In production since 2026-09-05: the digest is served publicly, production persistence has run, and the budget reservation in a transaction — the guardrail that could only be verified under real concurrent conditions — holds under 30 simultaneous reservations for 4 slots. The daily scheduler remains to be armed.
  • The recommendation. Make the pipeline observable before making it autonomous. The deployment diagnosis did not stumble on the infrastructure but on the fact that the pipeline logged nothing: under a scheduler, all the cost instrumentation built over the previous days would have vanished at the exact moment it becomes the only window on the system.

What follows details the scoping, the architecture and the evidence — in that order.

The problem

Manual export-risk monitoring cannot be justified after the fact

  • A scattered daily press review, unstructured email alerts
  • No trace of why a piece of information was kept or discarded
  • Audience: export compliance, competitive intelligence, public affairs functions

An explicit non-goal: the system does not decide. It speeds up and structures the human watch — the decision stays human.

Scoping

Three critical issues, not one

  1. Consistent classification of a heterogeneous stream of sources
  2. Multi-source cross-checking — telling a confirmed signal from an isolated rumour
  3. Systematic traceability — every statement backed by an identified source

1 and 3 covered in V1 · 2 delivered as the first V2 slice — perimeter extended to the five categories on 2026-08-20, escalation being conditioned on a measured candidate antecedent rather than on the category (see roadmap)

MECE perimeter

Scoped as MECE, then corrected on contact with real data

Included (V1)

Export control, arms contracts, military movements, defence diplomacy, industrial programmes — thematic filtering, worldwide.

Explicitly excluded

Classified intelligence, cybersecurity (a distinct neighbouring perimeter), automated decision-making, financial market analysis.

An accepted revision: strict geographic filtering, tested on real data, wrongly rejected nearly all of 2 sources out of 5 → removed, replaced by a location extraction kept as non-filtering metadata. Documented as a revision, not hidden.

Architecture

A traceable agentic pipeline, not a script

Sources

18 RSS feeds, selected by country, validated live

→
Collection

collector.py

→
Deduplication

before the LLM call, not after

→
Analysis

classification + summary + verified quote

→
Verification

cross-checking + confidence score, bounded tool loop

→
Grouping

event threads over the history, same bounded loop

Shared state (VigieState) between the nodes, every node traced natively — with no manual instrumentation. The first three nodes are a deterministic workflow; the last two let the model decide on its own tool calls, each under a coded double cap — one more agentic node is not one more cap to forget.

What survives runs — LLM budget, seen links, analysed history — goes through a single persistence layer, local files in development and Firestore in production. The digest served is a sliding window over that history, not a snapshot of the last run: since deduplication empties the later collections, serving the raw run would erase the display on every pass.

Display

The digest makes visible what commits confidence

The confidence score sits on a sequential ramp and not in red/green — a score read as a verdict would invalidate the non-goal. An item outside the verifier's perimeter comes out with no score rather than with a zero. The "state media" provenance is displayed, not buried.

Coverage

The map also displays what it cannot place

Built on the verified location of the event, not on the country of the source. Items with no extractable place and places not attachable to a country (maritime spaces, commands) are counted explicitly: a map that showed only its successes would overstate the real coverage.

Synthesis

The thread makes readable what happens between the articles

A thread links the articles of one story — same parties, same operation — and not of one theme. The timeline is at the real scale of time: the gap between publications is the signal, and equidistant markers would make three dispatches in twenty minutes and a story spread over three weeks look identical. No aggregate score is produced at thread level — averaging partly absent scores would fill by computation a gap left deliberately.

A non-negotiable guardrail

No summary without evidence

Every summary generated by the LLM must come with a quote verified verbatim against the source text. With no evidence, the item is rejected automatically — not merely flagged.

“The Rafale export deal was signed today.” — a source excerpt, never translated, never paraphrased

The same principle applies to the extracted place: empty rather than unverifiable.

Risks

Handled by priority, not by exhaustiveness

High priority

LLM hallucination

A verbatim quote is mandatory, with automatic rejection when absent.

High priority

Misuse

Explicit positioning: an aid to the watch, never an automated decision tool.

Medium

Source bias

Selection by country on a SIPRI criterion, state media marked as such in the digest.

Medium

LLM cost drift

A daily call cap + bounded agentic escalation — coded and tested, not merely documented.

Evidence from use · 1/2

The flattering figure was questioned before being kept

  • A guardrail declared, not wired. The daily LLM call cap existed in the config without being checked anywhere in the code. Found by self-audit, fixed, tested both ways.
  • Precision self-questioned. 27/30 agreement with the manual annotation, above target — kept only after questioning the annotation itself, which revealed ambiguous category definitions rather than a defect of the classifier.
  • A second, harsher measurement. After the sources were reworked: 73/88 (83%), below target. The annotation protocol itself carried a bias — documented in the scoping document rather than the figure being presented as settled.
  • A third measurement, decomposed. 51/68 (75%) on a rebuilt sample. The overall figure was the least informative: noise sorting meets the target (85%), qualifying what reaches the digest fails one time in three (64%). Two distinct problems a single figure conflated.
  • The retest contradicted the probe that had preceded it. A two-arm retest over 17 items: the reworded rule wins both its reference cases with no regression at all. But the controls — run this time, where the probe had stopped at two cases — found two earlier regressions, and a third arm attributed them to the previous day's change rather than to the rewording. The gain that change claimed does not reproduce: replayed four times on the earlier prompt, the case already comes out correctly. A probe at n=2 was comparing one observation to one observation.
  • A fourth measurement, blind. 37/48 (77%) — the annotation script had until then shown the system's verdict before asking for the human judgement, an anchoring bias removed the same day. At n=48 the 85% target falls inside the confidence interval: the measurement concludes neither way, and saying so beats quoting 77% as a verdict. It does, independently, confirm the boundary the previous one had pointed at.

A precision measurement tests the clarity of the definitions as much as the quality of the model. The two are audited together — and the audit corrected itself here: the scoping document stated nothing about the boundary in question, but the prompt had carried an undocumented rule for five days, which the model was faithfully applying. A disagreement about the specification and not a gap in it — the rule was changed, not added, and carried back into the scoping document. Probed straight after: effective on the case that motivated it, with no effect on a second one it nonetheless names explicitly. A rule can be written, correct, and move nothing. The full retest, since passed, corrected the second case — and showed that the effect claimed on the first was not reproducible. What remains open is no longer a question of rule but of annotation: two articles describing the same type of event carry opposite labels, and no wording can satisfy both.

Evidence from use · 2/2

The defects came from testing, not from re-reading

  • The real volume contradicted the estimate. Reworking the sources surfaced feeds exposing months of history. With no freshness window, the first run would have spent a whole day's budget on the backlog before stopping.
  • Symptom at display time, cause upstream. Only two countries were showing on the map. Rather than adding catch-up rules to the display: measure first. 10 of the 11 missing places had their country named in the title — yet the verbatim check only covered the body of the article, and was erasing correct extractions.
  • Fixed without weakening the guardrail. The title is text published by the source: the excerpt must still match it word for word. Coverage went from 6 to 13 places over 16 items.
  • A crash revealed the real loss. A model response outside the enumeration interrupted a run. On relaunch: zero new items — deduplication was marking links as seen before the analysis, so the items of the lost run were deemed processed while existing nowhere, and excluded from every subsequent collection. Marking now belongs to the node that actually paid for the call.

Trace the symptom back to the cause rather than fixing where the problem shows — and document what remains unverified rather than omitting it. None of these defects was visible in a code review.

Two pieces of work, in this order. The budget first. Extending the verifier to the five categories ran for real on 2026-08-21 (15 escalations over 36 articles, 5 with an antecedent) and saturated the day's cap of 200 calls — the shortfall fell on thread grouping, last in the chain. First real reading on 2026-08-22: analysis 72 calls, verification 50, grouping 73, that is 195 of the 200. It shows that the escalation caps are over-subscribed — together they authorise 140 calls, when analysis claims one per submitted article and consumed 72 — and that 21% of the budget goes on articles discarded after analysis. The arbitration stays open deliberately: it cannot be settled until that one-fifth share is broken down by source, and the breakdown has just been instrumented. The deployment next, and its repository half is done. The diagnosis had not stumbled on the absence of infrastructure but on the absence of a log: the pipeline emitted nothing, so the measurements above would have been invisible under a scheduler. Structured log, run endpoint closed behind a token, pinned dependencies, container image and daily batch job were delivered and validated locally on 2026-08-23 — the batch ran end to end against real feeds with the call cap forced to zero, which exercises truncation without spending anything. The cloud half followed on 2026-09-05, and the unknown is lifted: production persistence has run, and the budget reservation in a transaction — the guardrail that could be neither confirmed nor refuted anywhere but in production — holds under real concurrency, 30 simultaneous reservations across 3 containers for 4 slots, exactly 4 accepted. The method counted as much as the result: the runbook prescribed two full runs launched together, which produced a single reservation and did not even overlap — deduplication had starved them. A counter under the cap would have passed for proof. What remains is arming the scheduler, and observing the seven-day purge on the eighth day of history.

In summary

A complete scoping, applied to a real system — not just slides

MECE, alternatives assessed, KPIs, risks, governance — with the same demand for empirical verification from beginning to end, right down to the final precision measurement.
